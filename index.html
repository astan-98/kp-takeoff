Field Notes got an upgrade

Field Notes has a new home. Save your jobs, then open the new version.

Step 1

Export backup

Your saved jobs, drafts, work orders, prices and photos are stored on this phone in the current version. They don't move over on their own.

On iPhone, pick Save to Files, then iCloud Drive or On My iPhone.

Step 2

Open new app

On the new app, go to Library, tap Restore from backup, and pick the takeoff-backup file you just saved. Restoring adds to what's there and never deletes.

Open new app
Step 3

Re-add the home-screen icon

  1. In Safari on the new app, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Delete the old Field Notes icon.
  3. Re-enter your Ops key in Settings if the app asks.
